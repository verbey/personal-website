## Articles n' blog posts

* [Just Imagining a Workout Can Make You Stronger - Nautilus](https://nautil.us/just-imagining-a-workout-can-make-you-stronger-236068)
* [If people would just – Herman's blog](https://herman.bearblog.dev/if-people-would-just/)
	
	> Bad systems punish everyone for what a few might do: bank KYC and airport security are two I've had to deal with in the past week that annoyed me. They treat every person like a suspect, and punish them accordingly.
	>
	> Good systems work like a tree on a sidewalk. They don't rely on goodwill, and the people who were going to behave anyway barely even notice them.
	
	Herman strikes once again with the perfect observation.
	
*  [Climate: The 1.5°C goal of the Paris Agreement is no longer viable](https://unric.org/en/unep-emissions-gap-report-2026-1-5-degree-overshoot)

	No surprises here. Still disappointing.
	
* [My Laptop Stickers](https://joelchrono.xyz/blog/laptop-stickers)

	Stickers are a miracle for geek self-expression. I love the ones I have on my ThinkPad.
	
* [Why Are Coding Agents So Dumb? · mtlynch.io](https://mtlynch.io/why-are-coding-agents-so-dumb)

	The points raised are spot on. Nevertheless, I find it funny how the dream agent described is just an SWE... An LLM can generate code en masse, yet even top harnesses plan poorly, don't produce diagrams (unless explicitly asked) etc — everything that an experienced dev does without guidance from upper management.
	
* [Radxa's Q8B has 2x the performance and expansion of the Pi 5 - Jeff Geerling](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2026/radxa-q8b-double-pi-5)
	I don't have anything against SBCs, but those prices are befuddling: for that price, you can *easily* grab a used mini PC that might not have an NPU and two 2.5 Gbit Ethernet ports but will totally crush the SBC when it comes to CPU, available USB, HDMI/DP ports etc.

* [The lamps in my house](https://arslan.io/2026/10/05/the-lamps-in-my-house)

	There's a soft spot in my heart for posts like that. People collecting things, sharing their hobbies, obsessions and being blatantly honest about it being an obsession:
	
	> Are they worth it? Probably not for their price, but for me they are worth every penny. At a certain point, it becomes more like collecting, and collecting things is very different from just having a lamp to illuminate a room
It's amazing, I've never noticed people paying so much attention to their lamps before.
